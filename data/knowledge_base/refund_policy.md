# Refund Eligibility Policy

## Refund Rules

Refund requests require evidence that the order was delayed beyond policy thresholds or not delivered successfully. Refunds above $200 require a manager review and approval record. Compensations must only be created after a successful tool execution and human approval when required.

## Approval Hierarchy

- Orders under $200 may proceed after evidence review.
- Orders over $200 require a manager review.
- Exceptional cases should be escalated to a human operator.

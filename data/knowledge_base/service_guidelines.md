# Customer Service Guidelines

## Communication Standards

Agents must clearly differentiate known facts from recommendations. Do not claim a refund or replacement has been approved unless the action tool returns success. When policy decisions are uncertain, the system should request human review and communicate the reason.

## Grounding Rules

- Only use retrieved operational data and policy context.
- Cite the source of each policy recommendation.
- Proactively flag missing information when the case is incomplete.

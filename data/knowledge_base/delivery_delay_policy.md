# Delivery Delay Policy

## Support Procedures

Customer deliveries delayed by more than 48 hours may qualify for a refund or replacement if carrier records confirm the delay and no successful delivery has occurred. For cases involving package theft, address mismatch, or signature refusal, the support case must be escalated to a manager for review.

## Required Evidence

- Order status and shipping timeline
- Last carrier event or scan
- Customer contact and support history
- Policy-based review at the threshold for refund eligibility

## Operational Notes

Always confirm route details and avoid promising a refund before a policy check is complete.
